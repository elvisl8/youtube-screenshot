📸 YouTube Screenshot Settings

All three ways to capture work the same: the camera button at the bottom right of the player, the keyboard shortcut, and the button in the toolbar popup. Capturing does not pause playback; the video keeps playing and the image is the frame at the moment you pressed.

Notes and limits

DRM-protected video can't be captured: for DRM content such as Netflix, Disney+ and paid YouTube movies, the browser blacks out the picture. This is an industry-wide restriction that no extension can get around. Regular YouTube videos (including 4K) are not affected.
Subtitles: clean frames never contain subtitles (they are overlaid by the web page and are not part of the video picture). To include subtitles, use "What you see", turn subtitles on, and capture in fullscreen.
  • Shorts pages work too (native vertical resolution).
  • You can capture while paused; you get the frame that is on screen.
  • Safari: this extension is standard Web Extension source and can be converted with Xcode's safari-web-extension-converter; see the Safari section of the README in the package.
